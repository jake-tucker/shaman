Shaman's mark: five candidates

Throwaway prototype for map ticket #821. Every macOS picture is what ictool renders from the candidate's .icon at that size; every Windows and Linux picture is sips on its SVG at that size. Nothing is scaled by the browser but the rows that say so. The purple of the tinted forms is ictool's own; a person's Mac tints with the colour they chose. Press a column, or ← and →, to see one candidate whole.
